Our approach(01)

Most people don’t dread the dentist. They dread not knowing what’s about to happen. So we explain everything, before anything begins.

  1. i.

    We explain first

    Before any treatment, we show you what we’ve found and talk through what it means, in plain language and without rushing.

  2. ii.

    You set the pace

    We agree a simple signal before we start. Raise it at any point and we stop, no questions asked.

  3. iii.

    Choices, not pressure

    Where there’s more than one sensible option, you’ll hear about each one, including its costs and what it asks of you.

Your visit(03)

What a visit feels like.

From the moment you arrive to the moment you leave, you’ll know what’s happening and why.

  1. Before

    Tell us what’s on your mind

    Let us know what you’d like help with and anything that worries you, so we can plan the right amount of time.

  2. Arrival

    A conversation first

    We start sitting upright and talking, not reclined in the chair. You’ll know what we plan to do before we do it.

  4. During

    Your pace, your signal

    We check in as we go and explain each step. Use your signal to pause at any time.

  5. Afterwards

    A clear next step

    You’ll leave knowing what we found, the options we discussed and what, if anything, happens next.

    Book an appointment
Questions(04)

Good questions, straight answers.

Can’t see your question? Send us a message with your appointment request.

How do I book my first appointment?

Fill in the appointment request form on our contact page. The team will contact you to agree a time that suits you. Your appointment is only confirmed once you’ve heard back from us.

I’m nervous. Can you help?

Yes. Mention it when you get in touch and we’ll plan extra time. Your first visit can simply be a conversation, and you can pause at any point with a signal we agree together.

What happens at a first appointment?

We’ll ask about your dental and general health and what you’d like help with, then examine your teeth, gums and mouth. Afterwards we explain what we found and talk through any options.

How much will my treatment cost?

It depends on what you need, so we can’t give a reliable figure until we’ve seen you. After your examination we’ll explain your options and their costs before any treatment begins.

I’m in pain. What should I do?

Contact us as early in the day as you can and we’ll do our best to help. If you have swelling that affects your breathing, swallowing or eye, or bleeding that won’t stop, call 999 or go to A&E. Outside our hours, NHS 111 can advise you.

Can I change or cancel an appointment?

Of course. Please let us know as soon as you can, so we can offer the time to someone else and find a new one for you.
