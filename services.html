General & preventive(02)

Where most visits begin.

Routine care helps you and your clinician understand how your teeth and gums are doing, and spot anything worth keeping an eye on early.

Check-ups & examinations

A thorough look at your teeth, gums and mouth, with time to ask questions. We’ll tell you what’s healthy, what to keep an eye on and whether anything needs attention.

Worth booking if you’re due a routine visit, haven’t been for a while, or something feels different.

Request a check-up

Hygiene appointments

Gentle, careful cleaning to remove build-up and support healthy gums, plus practical advice you can use at home.

Worth booking if you’d like a professional clean, or want advice on looking after your gums.

Request a hygiene visit
Cosmetic & straightening(03)

Changes to your smile, considered carefully.

Cosmetic treatment starts with an honest conversation about what’s realistic for your teeth, so you can decide whether it’s right for you.

Teeth whitening

A natural-looking brightening of your own teeth. We’ll first check whether whitening suits you and explain what result is realistic.

Ask about whitening

Clear aligners

Discreet, removable aligners that straighten teeth gradually. We’ll assess whether they’re right for you and explain the commitment involved, including how long they’re worn each day.

Ask about aligners
Restorative(04)

Replacing a missing tooth, one step at a time.

Dental implants

A long-term way to replace a missing tooth. Because every mouth is different, implants always start with a detailed consultation, and you’ll understand each stage, the likely timeline and the costs before deciding anything.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We talk about what you’d like, look at your teeth and gums, and discuss whether an implant could be suitable.

  2. 02

    Your options, explained

    If an implant is an option, we explain what’s involved, alongside any alternatives, with costs set out clearly.

  3. 03

    Your decision

    There’s no pressure to go ahead. Take the time you need, and come back with any questions.

Book an implant consultation
Urgent care(05)

In pain? Get in touch early.

Urgent appointments

Toothache, a chipped tooth or a lost filling? Call us on +357 22 445 210 as early in the day as you can and we’ll do our best to see you promptly.

Seek emergency care if you have swelling that affects your breathing, swallowing or eye, or bleeding that won’t stop: call 999 or go to A&E. Outside our hours, NHS 111 can advise you.

How we plan your care(06)

Whatever brings you in, the approach is the same: understand first, then decide together.

  • Assessment before advice

    We look properly and listen first, so any recommendation is based on you rather than a standard plan.

  • Options, not one answer

    Where there’s more than one sensible route, including doing nothing for now, you’ll hear about each one.

  • Costs before commitment

    We explain your options and their costs before any treatment begins, so there are no surprises.

Questions(07)

Choosing the right appointment.

Still unsure? Send an appointment request and tell us what’s on your mind.

How do I decide which appointment to book?

If you’re not sure, a check-up is usually the best place to start. On the request form you can also choose “Not sure yet” and describe what you’d like help with; the team will suggest the right kind of appointment when they contact you.

Can I ask about other treatments during my appointment?

Yes. If something else is on your mind, mention it. We’ll talk it through, and if it needs more time we can arrange a separate visit to discuss it properly.

Do I need a consultation before cosmetic or implant treatment?

Yes. We always assess your teeth and talk through what’s realistic before recommending any cosmetic or implant treatment, so you can decide with all the information in front of you.

How much will my treatment cost?

It depends on what you need, so we can’t give a reliable figure until we’ve seen you. After your examination we’ll explain your options and their costs before any treatment begins.

How can I contact the clinic with a question?

Call us on +357 22 445 210, email hello@calderdental.com.cy, or add your question to the message field on the appointment request form.
