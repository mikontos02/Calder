Our story(02)

A practice built on conversation.

Calder exists for a simple reason: a dental appointment shouldn’t leave you guessing.

Plenty of people put off the dentist, not because of the treatment itself, but because of how it feels to sit in the chair without knowing what’s happening or why. Calder is designed to work differently.

To us, good dentistry is calm and unhurried. It means listening before examining, explaining before treating, and leaving room for whatever is on your mind.

The relationship between you and your clinician matters as much as the care itself. When you understand your options, you can make decisions you feel good about.

So you can expect honest advice, a clear plan and the same attention to detail, whether you’re here for a check-up or a long-considered change.

Our philosophy(03)

Four principles, in every appointment.

They shape how we speak to you, how we plan your care and how we work as a team.

  1. 01

    Patient-first care

    You deserve to feel heard, respected and comfortable from the moment you get in touch. Your concerns shape the appointment, not the other way round.

  2. 02

    Clarity and communication

    We explain what we see and what your options are in plain language, so every decision is an informed one, and yours to make.

  3. 03

    Precision and attention to detail

    Careful work starts with careful looking. We give each step the time it needs and consider what’s right for you specifically.

  4. 04

    A considered approach to modern dentistry

    Contemporary techniques are there to serve your care. Where a newer approach genuinely helps, we’ll tell you why. Where it doesn’t, we won’t push it.

Our approach(06)

What a visit here feels like in practice.

  1. We start with your concerns

    Before we look at anything, we ask what’s brought you in and what you’d like from the visit.

  2. You’ll see what we see

    Where it helps, we’ll show you on a model, a picture or a diagram, so the explanation actually makes sense.

  3. Questions are part of the appointment

    There’s no such thing as a silly question, and you won’t be hurried out of the chair before you’ve asked it.

  4. Care that fits the person

    Recommendations are based on your teeth, your priorities and your circumstances, never a one-size-fits-all plan.

  5. Booking that’s straightforward

    Send a request, we’ll call to agree a time, and you’ll know what your first visit involves before you arrive.

Getting started(07)

Three steps to your first visit.

  1. 01

    Get in touch

    Send an appointment request or contact the clinic, and tell us what you’d like help with.

  2. 02

    Have a conversation

    We’ll talk through your needs and answer your questions, at whatever pace suits you.

  3. 03

    Understand your options

    If anything needs attention, we’ll explain the possible next steps so you can decide what’s right for you.
