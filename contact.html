Questions(03)

Before you get in touch.

Anything else? Add it to the message field on your appointment request.

Is my appointment booked when I send the form?

Not yet. The form sends a request. A member of the team will contact you to agree a date and time, and your appointment is confirmed once you’ve both agreed it.

Can I ask for a particular date or time?

Yes. Choose a preferred date and time of day on the form, or mention the days that suit you in your message. We’ll do our best to find something that works, though we can’t promise a specific slot until we’ve spoken.

What if I’m not sure which treatment I need?

Choose “Not sure yet” and tell us a little about what’s on your mind. We’ll suggest the right kind of appointment. You can also read about our treatments first.

I’m in pain. Should I use the form?

Choose “Urgent appointment” so the team knows it’s urgent, and call us on +357 22 445 210 too. If you have swelling that affects your breathing, swallowing or eye, or bleeding that won’t stop, call 999 or go to A&E. Outside our hours, NHS 111 can advise you.
